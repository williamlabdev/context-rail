## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for credentials, private data, IAM, deployment or other security-sensitive findings. Report them through the Project's private security contact: `<REPLACE_ME_SECURITY_CONTACT>`.

Include the affected version/commit, reproduction steps, impact and any safe mitigation. Never include secrets or real customer data in an issue, Request, DecisionRecord or Evidence Bundle.
