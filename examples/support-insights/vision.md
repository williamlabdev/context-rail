# Project Vision

## Problem

Support leads need a consistent view of recurring ticket themes and response-time trends without changing ticket source data.

## Users and outcome

- Primary users: support leads and delivery managers.
- Desired outcome: a read-only weekly report that makes trends and evidence easy to review.
- Success measure: every reported metric links to a source snapshot and reporting period.

## Non-goals

- automatic customer communication;
- editing or deleting tickets;
- production deployment in this fixture.
